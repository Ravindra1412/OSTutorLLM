A student just finished a quiz on {topic}. Their score was {score_percent}%. They got {correct_count} of {total_count} questions right.

Their weakest areas in this attempt were: {weak_question_summaries}

Write brief, encouraging feedback (3-5 sentences) that:
1. Acknowledges what they did well first.
2. Names the specific concept(s) behind their mistakes, not just "you got some wrong."
3. Gives one concrete, actionable suggestion for what to review next.
Do not repeat the raw score number back to them (they already see it on screen) — focus on the qualitative takeaway.
